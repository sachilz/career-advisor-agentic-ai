# Comprehensive Software Engineering Interview Preparation Guide

## Preparation Strategy for Technical Interviews

### 1. Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA)
- Master fundamental topics: Arrays, Linked Lists, Stacks, Queues, Binary Trees, Graphs, Hash Tables, and Sorting algorithms.
- Practice solving LeetCode/HackerRank problems (Easy to Medium difficulty) covering:
  - Two Pointers & Sliding Window techniques.
  - Recursion and Backtracking.
  - Breadth-First Search (BFS) and Depth-First Search (DFS).
  - Dynamic Programming basics.

### 2. System Design & Architectural Concepts
- Understand client-server communication, load balancing, caching strategies (Redis), CDN, database sharding, and message queues.
- Be prepared to discuss scalability, trade-offs, and microservice architectures.

### 3. Behavioral Interviews & STAR Method
- Structure answers to behavioral questions using the **STAR** method:
  - **S**ituation: Context of the problem or task.
  - **T**ask: What needed to be achieved.
  - **A**ction: The specific technical steps you took.
  - **R**esult: Quantifiable output or achievement.
- Be prepared to explain final year university projects, GitHub code, and team collaboration challenges.
